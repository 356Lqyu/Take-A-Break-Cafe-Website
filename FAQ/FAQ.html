About Our Brand

As the core of take a break cafe,our mission is to give busy people a break and bring expensive specialty coffee to the local community at the most affordable price. We strive to make intriguing coffee and strive to provide maximum convenience to the community. Also make sure that taking a break cafe is always a necessity rather than a luxury.

If you want to order your favorite drink, you can do it in-store or with a few taps on our TAKE A BREAK CAFE app. Download our TAKE A BREAK CAFE app via the app on Google Play or the App Store and order now!
